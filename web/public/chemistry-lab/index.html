How to use the lab

  1. Set up: drag glassware (test tubes, flasks, burette, Bunsen burner, water bath) from Apparatus onto the bench.
  2. Add chemicals: drag a bottle from Chemicals onto a container, then choose an amount: “a few drops” (dropwise), 1–5 cm³ (“in excess”), a spatula of solid, or 25.0 cm³ by pipette.
  3. Pour / mix: drag one container onto another. If a tube is giving off a gas, you can bubble the gas into another tube (e.g. lime water).
  4. Heat: drag a container onto the Bunsen burner and click the burner to light it. Warm flammable liquids in the water bath instead.
  5. Test gases: drag tools (damp litmus, splints, glass rods dipped in conc. HCl/NH₃, “waft to smell”) onto a tube while it is fizzing. Use the nichrome wire for flame tests: dip it in a sample, then drag it into a lit flame.
  6. Titrate: drag a solution onto the burette to fill it, drag a conical flask under the tip, then click the burette and use the tap buttons. Click “Record end point” and enter your readings in the Titration table.
  7. Record: every action is written into the Observations table (Test · Observation · Inference). In missions, write your own inferences, then answer the questions in Report.
  8. Click any bottle to read its uses, hazards and key reactions. In the Free lab, the Inspector also shows the ions, pH and temperature (like ChemCollective’s Solution Info panel).
