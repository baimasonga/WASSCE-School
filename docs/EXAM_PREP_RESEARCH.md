# Certification exam-preparation sellers: research notes

Checked 4 November 2026. This is a focused product comparison, not a complete market ranking. Prices are the displayed USD prices at the time of research, before any checkout taxes or later changes.

## Popularity finding

ExamTopics is a prominent question-bank site. Semrush estimates 1.05 million visits to examtopics.com in September 2026. That is an estimate of visits (not unique users, paid subscribers, sales, or evidence that it is globally number one). The same page lists Whizlabs at about 263,780 visits. No comparable complete ranking across all providers was established.

- Traffic source: https://www.semrush.com/website/examtopics.com/overview/
- Official site: https://www.examtopics.com/

The official ExamTopics homepage was discoverable in search, but direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. Current Contributor Access/PDF prices were not reliably verifiable from official pages. Competing sellers report conflicting prices; those claims are excluded here. No interoperability or export rights were verified. Similar-looking domains are not treated as the official service.

## Recommendation (product judgment)

Use a free core bank plus paid subject/year packs or an annual all-subject subscription. Fit the pricing to Sierra Leone learners after testing willingness to pay; international IT-certification prices are not a local pricing recommendation. Differentiate through offline downloads, worked solutions, syllabus coverage and progress tracking. Publish original questions or licensed past papers.

The implemented Android importer is the offline content delivery foundation. It does not log in to these services, import their proprietary products, or establish that a learner has paid. Production subscription delivery requires a seller-owned backend: subscriber authentication, server-side purchase validation, entitlements, signed pack downloads and a defined offline renewal/grace period. Billing providers, pricing and backend hosting remain decisions for a later implementation.
